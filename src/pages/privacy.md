# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

This privacy policy explains how Yancheng Xuanbao Environmental Technology Co., Ltd.
("Xuanbao", "we") handles information on this website.

## Information You Send Us

When you contact us through the inquiry form or by email, you share the information you
choose to provide — typically your name, company, email address and details of your gas
treatment requirements. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and to
prepare technical assessments and quotations.

## Cookies & Analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. Where analytics
are enabled, they are used to understand aggregate site usage.

## Hosting & Infrastructure

This website is a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider may
process technical access logs for security and performance purposes.

## Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only as necessary to
respond to your inquiry, or where required by law.
